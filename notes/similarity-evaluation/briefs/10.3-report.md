## Choices, as made

### Part A

1. **`RECONCILE_MOST_UNKNOWNS` (20)**: `candidates_of` raises `TooManyUnknowns` (a `ValueError`, in the root's
   `__all__`) before any part is asked when more unknowns are asked about than the cap: all the plan's, or with
   `only` those of `only`. The POST maps it to a 422 whose detail is the message ("The document has 2 unknowns,
   and at most 1 are asked about at once (RECONCILE_MOST_UNKNOWNS)"). `RECONCILE_FETCH` is from 1 to 1,000. Both
   checked at `initialise()` (one helper, `_whole`, says the bounds), tests by message.
2. **`only` and the cap**: the parts are asked of every unknown of the plan while the plan has at most the cap;
   above it, of those of `only` alone (`asked = unknowns if len(unknowns) <= most else wanted`). Tested: a stored
   document of two unknowns at a cap of 1 asks the parts of one (and of two at 20).
3. **Both routes need a login**: the GET has `dependencies=[Depends(get_current_user)]`, as the vectors' status
   route. Tests: 401 on both routes for a reader who is not logged in, the parts asked 0 times and `nearest` 0
   times (both counted by patching `pangloss_vectors.parts.unknown_vectors` and `pangloss_vectors.search.nearest`);
   below the routes, `stored_plan` as a reader with `see_unpublished` False gives None for a stand-in of an
   unpublished document and a plan for one of a published document.
4. **The 500s of the GET**: a head that is an entity is read with `get_for_edit` (as review reads an entity it
   writes again), a document with `get`. `ValidationError` and `PanglossWriteRefused` while making the update and
   the database model are `stored.Unplannable`; the route catches it and `PanglossReferenceError` (from the parts),
   logs a warning on `pangloss.reconcile` ("The candidates of {id} cannot be found: {reason}") and answers 404 with
   one message: "The document of the stand-in {id} cannot be planned as it is stored". Three tests by patched
   raiser: `pangloss_models.utils.editable` giving `{}` (the `Update` refuses), `write_functions.to_db_model`
   raising `PanglossWriteRefused`, `parts.unknown_vectors` raising `PanglossReferenceError` (standing for the graph
   part's resolution, which is reached through it). No test of a stand-in below a real entity head: the suite's
   models have none.
5. **Scores**: (a) weights that leave no part the profile weighs above 0 are refused; (b) a part whose vector of
   the unknown is all zeros is not scored and not in the sum below the line (it was searched by neither before);
   (c) a candidate's vector of another length counts 0 for that part, as if it had none. Pure tests.

### Part B (reasons)

6. **`_resolved` moved** to `pangloss_cypher.validate_write.resolved_plan` (with its URL query, `URL_IDS_QUERY`);
   `pangloss_fastrp.part._resolved` is now a thin async wrapper that imports it lazily (as it did its parts), so
   the graph part's code and tests are as they were. `reasons.shared` reads it in its own read transaction.
7. **The entities of the payload**: every `ReferenceToExisting` (a URL as the node it names) and every entity
   written in the payload that exists, of the resolved plan (merged ones are their survivors). A candidate is never
   counted as sharing itself (`xid <> cid`).
8. **The query** (one statement for every candidate of a request, `reasons._query`): for each entity, the heads of
   the nodes with a forward edge to it (`collect(DISTINCT coalesce(n2.head_node_id, n2.id))`), collected as two
   parallel lists (not a list of maps: the Memgraph trap with maps made over collected values); then for each
   candidate the heads of the nodes with a forward edge to it, each a `HeadNode` that is a `Document`, `listed`,
   `counted` (not a derived document), not the plan's own head; each pair counted where the head is in the entity's
   list. Forward edges by `fragments.forward` and `fragments.reference` (as `_used`). The shared entity `listed`.
9. **Cut**: at most `RECONCILE_REASONS` (5) a candidate, most documents first, then id; each a reference view with
   `documents`; `[]` when none; 0 asks nothing (no transaction). The score is unchanged (tested).
10. **The budget moves by one transaction and two statements, not one statement**: the reasons are a read
    transaction of their own with the plan's references checked as a write resolves them (`check_references_exist`:
    one statement here; more with URLs or entities written in the payload) and the query. Now 14 transactions and
    59 statements; with `RECONCILE_REASONS = 0`, 13 and 57 (the test runs both, so the difference is held).
11. **After a merge** the documents that named the absorbed entity have their forward edges on the survivor (none
    is left to the absorbed one from a node of the models; only its `PGCreation` record's edge), and a payload that
    names the absorbed one is read as naming the survivor: the survivor is shared, in the documents of both (tested:
    3 = 2 of Ben's and the one that named Eve).
12. **The world of the live file gained places** (the mill, the ford, as `InRole.at`), for "shares the place".
