# Report: step 7a.1, a burst refreshed in groups

Started 2026-11-21 by the builder of 7a.1. Choices are written here as they are made.

## Choices

1. **The remembering reader is a subclass**, `RememberingReader(CypherReader)` in `cypher_reader.py`, made by
   `reading(maps, work, remember=N)` / `in_thread(..., remember=N)` (N: the most nodes it holds before it forgets;
   `None`, the default, gives the plain `CypherReader` as before, so `overlay_plan` and `approximately` are
   unchanged). The bound is `REMEMBERED_CAPS = 10` (in `cypher_reader.py`); `refresh_ids` passes
   `REMEMBERED_CAPS * cap`. It counts the nodes it remembers (ids asked of `nodes`, found or not) and checks the
   bound before each read: past it, all five caches are emptied (`seen` is kept, as now). So the bound is soft by
   at most one call's worth.
2. **`CITING_QUERIES` and `BELOW_QUERY` themselves now also return `id AS of`** (the unwound id the row answers:
   the cited id, the head), rather than separate copies for the remembering reader. The plain reader ignores the
   column; the plan is the same (the column is the UNWIND variable, equal to `c.target_id` / `n.head_node_id`).
3. **A unit** is `Unit(id, kind)` (a `NamedTuple`, kind `"written"`, `"removed"` or `"before"`), so sorted order is
   by id, then kind. A head removed that is also written is given as written only (as `refresh_ids` did: `heads` was
   the union). An id may be both a head and a `before` id: two units.
4. **The grouping** is `in_groups(units, attempt)` in `part.py`, pure and generic: it keeps the order it is given
   (the caller sorts), tries the whole, halves on `None` (`units[: n // 2]`, then the rest), depth first, and gives
   `(results in the order made, units that failed alone)`. No units: no attempt.
5. **When groups disagree** (possible only on Neo4j, where a read sees later commits, and only for an id read again
   after the reader forgot): the later group's answer for a stop wins (a vector replaces a gone, a gone a vector).
6. **What `approximately` is told is exact**: every stop an exact group answered, with a vector *or* as gone (both
   are the rebuild's answer). It neither writes, removes nor counts them, and treats them as neighbours (their
   stored vectors, written just before, are read), not as stops worked out with the approximate ones.
7. **`Refreshed.written` is now the exact rows only** (the brief's point 7). The approximate path used to return
   `written = stale = len(rows)`; it now returns `written = 0`. One existing live test asserted `(2, 2)` for a
   single head past the cap; changed to `(0, 2)`.
8. **The pure exactness test** (`test_a_batch_refreshed_in_groups_equals_a_rebuild`) makes a batch of 1 to 4 random
   edits of `World` per round (10 rounds), and finds its units the way the worker would: a head (document or person)
   whose tree (ids, nodes, edges) differs is written, one no longer there is removed, and `before` is what the trees
   of the changed and removed heads were and were joined to before. Each group's centre is the real
   `part.changed_stops` over the world's graph with a `below` (`WithBelow`). It also asserts that the centres
   together hold everything whose joins changed (`changed_between`). "first"/"last": the removed and `before` units
   are the whole first (last) group, the written heads shuffled over the other groups. Checked that it bites: with
   the `before` units dropped from the centres, all 18 cases fail.
9. **The live cap that forces a split** (`CAP_THAT_SPLITS = 30`, second live test): found by trying caps on
   `write_shapes`' data. The batch (a delete of "no gift" and the update of "meeting" that drops Eve and the place)
   needs 35 stops whole; at 30 it halves three levels deep and every group fits; at 25 a unit is past it alone. The
   test asserts the first attempt was too large and that later ones fitted, so a change of the shapes that stops
   the split fails loudly. The hub test uses the cap of 50 (the hub of sixty transfers is past it alone; the eleven
   far factoids are 4 stops each, 44 together, so even all eleven in one group fit).
10. **Docs beyond the brief's list**, because they became untrue: `implementation_docs/vectors.md` ("Past the cap"
    and the "Measured" sentence that said grouping was for the user to decide), `guide/vectors/README.md` ("Hubs":
    a burst no longer leaves everything approximate), and the anchor of the renamed section in
    `implementation_docs/graph-embeddings.md` and `vectors.md`. In `fastrp.md` the "Open" bullet is struck through
    as decided and built; `TODO.md` is untouched.
