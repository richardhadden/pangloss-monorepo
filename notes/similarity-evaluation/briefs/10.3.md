## Part A: mends (a fresh agent read the package and asked: is anything written, is anything shown that may not be)

Nothing written, nothing leaked. To mend:

1. **Unbounded work.** A setting `RECONCILE_MOST_UNKNOWNS` (20): a plan with more unknowns is refused before any
   part is asked (the POST: 422 saying how many it has and the most; `candidates_of` raises a `ValueError`
   subclass the route maps; with `only`, the count is of those asked for, but see 2). `RECONCILE_FETCH` at most
   1,000. Both checked at `initialise()`, with tests by message.
2. **`only` still lays out every unknown of the document**, so the stored route's cost grows with the document.
   Keep it (each unknown laid out as the others are is the point), but the cap of 1 applies to the plan's whole
   count there too: a stored document with more unknowns than the cap answers for the one asked with the parts
   asked **of that one alone** (`unknown_vectors(plan, [that one])`): say so in the docstring and `reconcile.md`.
3. **Both routes need a login.** The GET was open to anyone and is not cheap; reconciling is work of people logged
   in (review's router is login-only). Use the dependency the vectors' status route uses. The tests of a reader
   who is not logged in become: 401 on both routes, nothing computed (count the searches); keep one test **below
   the route** (`stored_plan` and `candidates_of` called as a reader who may not see unpublished documents) that
   an unpublished document's stand-in gives None, so the rule is still held if a route is opened later.
4. **A 500 on the GET.** `model.Update(**editable(view.model_dump()))` can raise pydantic's `ValidationError`
   (a stand-in below an entity head: `model.get` gives a `HeadView`; find whether `get_for_edit` is what should be
   read, as review's `give_uris` reads an entity, and use it where the head is an entity), `to_db_model` and
   `fill_labels` can raise a `PanglossWriteRefused`, the graph part's `_resolved` a `PanglossReferenceError`. A
   stored document cannot be "refused": answer 404 with the one message for a stand-in whose document cannot be
   planned, log a warning with the reason, test each of the three with a patched raiser.
5. **Scores.** (a) `RECONCILE_WEIGHTS` that leaves no part above 0 among those the profile weighs: refused at
   `initialise()`. (b) A part whose vector of the unknown is all zeros is as if the part had said nothing: not in
   `scores`, not in the denominator. (c) A stored vector of another dimension than the unknown's (a part scored
   that the profile's `rebuild_due` does not cover): that candidate has no score of that part (as if it had no
   vector), never a `ValueError`. Pure tests for each.
