a live test **seen failing before it** (say so for each), on both backends.

   `taken` Jon; the record removed). S is restored to the suggestion, and Bob confirms it as someone else, or with
   alternatives open: `confirmed` finds no record and returns; the example still says Jon, and the sweep keeps it
