## The cases

- `fold`: case; accents (`Zürich`, `Straße`, `Łukasz`); punctuation and more than one space; a hyphenated name is
  two words; digits kept; an underscore is no letter; the empty string and a string of punctuation give no words.
- Word order: `"Smith, John"` and `"John Smith"` give the same vector (`vector`), exactly.
- `canonical` and `variants_table`: a variant is replaced, another word is not; the table is folded (a variant
  given as `Jörg` is found for `jorg`); a variant equal to its own canonical form is no entry; refused with
  `ValueError`: a variant of two canonical forms, a variant that is itself a canonical form of another, a form or a
  variant of two words.
- `trigrams`: `ann` gives `^an`, `ann`, `nn$`; a word of one letter gives one; the empty word none.
- `words`: counts across several names of one item; with the table, the canonical form is what is counted.
- `Rarity.of`: counts an item once for a word however often it has it; an item with no words is not counted among
  `names`; a word only one item has is not kept; `weight` is 1 for a word every item has, greater the fewer have it,
  and the same for a word one item has, a word none has, and a word the table never saw.
- `weighted`: every trigram of a word carries the word's weight; a trigram two words of the item share carries both.
- A name and its variant spelling are nearer than two different names (cosine of `vector`; e.g. `Johannes Mayer`
  against `Johannes Maier`, against `Wilhelm Schuster`); with the table, a name and its variant (`Hans Mayer`,
  `Johannes Mayer`) are the same vector.
- A rare name outweighs a common one: in a made-up population where `johannes` is in most names and each surname in
  few, `Johannes Mayer` is nearer `Wilhelm Mayer` than `Johannes Schuster`; with a `Rarity()` that knows nothing
  (every word weighs the same) it is not so (the two are then about as near).
- Hashing is the same in any process: `place` of a few trigrams equals values written into the test (compute them
  once and write them in), and a subprocess (`sys.executable -c ...`, with another `PYTHONHASHSEED`) gives the same
  vector for a name. The sign is used: some trigram of a small sample has each sign.
- `hashed`: the dimension asked is the length; two trigrams at one place add or cancel by their signs.
- `vector`: at length one; None for an empty name, for punctuation alone, for no names; a plain string is taken as
  one name, not as its letters.
