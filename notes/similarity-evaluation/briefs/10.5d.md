## What it does, in one paragraph

A save of a document that may hold a stand-in leaves a mark of the kind `reconcile` when the write says so
(`suggest=true`) or the project's setting does and the write does not say otherwise. The worker claims marked
heads; for each stand-in of a head that sits where a suggestion fits, it asks the candidates as the stored
route does, decides (`suggest.py`), and writes **one update** of the document, as a toolchain user, in which
each stand-in decided is replaced by a suggestion (`IdentificationByToolchainFromText`: the stand-in's label as
`text`, its URIs, the alternatives with their per cent). A stand-in not decided stays, and is not asked again
until something that would change the answer has changed. `pangloss-reconcile` still imports neither review nor
the library: it finds stand-ins by `unknowns_of`/`stored.py` and writes the class a setting names.

## What to build

### 1. What sets a mark (the least of 10.5e, needed to test the worker)

  `RECONCILE_SUGGEST_AS` (`"IdentificationByToolchainFromText"`: the name of a model; checked **only when the
  setting on-save is True or when the worker runs** that it is a model with the fields the worker fills:
  `text`, `uris`, `identified_by`, `toolchain_version`, `target`; a project without the library and with the
  setting off is not refused), `RECONCILE_TOOLCHAIN` (`"pangloss-reconcile"`: a username),
  `RECONCILE_WORKER_IN_SERVER` (True).
- `hooks.declare_write_instruction(WriteInstruction("suggest", ("true", "false"), ...))` and a function of
  `hooks.instructed_work`, installed with the app: for a class of head **that people write and that can hold a
  stand-in** (worked out once from the models after `initialise()`: a head with an `Update`, not a derived
  document, some field of which, at any depth, takes a class of `hooks.stand_ins`; say how you found "people
  write it": row 25): `suggest` said `"true"` → `{KIND: True}`; said `"false"` → `{KIND: False}` (a standing mark
  taken off: row 7); **not said** → `{KIND: True}` if `RECONCILE_SUGGEST_ON_SAVE`, else `{}` (**not** `False`:
  a save that says nothing must not take off a mark a trigger left). Any other class: `{}`.
- `KIND = "reconcile"`.

### 2. The worker (`pangloss_reconcile/suggesting.py`; `suggest.py` stays pure)

One worker for a database, by a lease on a node of its own, as the vectors worker; **few heads claimed at a time
and the lease renewed while it works** (a mention asked takes 1 to 2.5 s at size; row 15). For each claimed head,
in this order, cheapest first:

1. **Is it to be touched at all.** The head read once (`rewriting.read_head`). Left alone, **its mark completed
   and the reason logged at info** (never tried again until marked again), when: it is gone or deleted; it is
   **published** (the user: only unpublished documents; find how the core says published, as
   `fragments.published` reads it, from what the view gives); its class is not one people write; its stored
   form cannot be planned (`stored.Unplannable`).
2. **Its stand-ins**, from its stored plan read **once** (`stored.py` gains a plan by head; today it plans by a
   node's id) and `unknowns_of`. Each is skipped when it was **tried with today's signatures** (below).
3. **By trial, which shape its place takes** (rows 8 and 9), with `rewriting.prepare_rewrite` and nothing
   written: the stand-in replaced by a `RECONCILE_SUGGEST_AS` whose target is (a) a `WeightedAlternative` of one
   placeholder entity at 100 per cent, then (b) one placeholder entity; the placeholder is a reference to an
   entity of a class the stand-in stands for (`StandIn.named_type`, as `unknowns_of` gives it), which an
   `Update`'s validation does not look up. The first that validates is the shape; if neither does, the stand-in
   is left (the field does not take a suggestion there) and **noted as tried**. The relation's own properties
   (`edge_properties`) are kept on the replacement as review's reconciliation keeps them. Reconcile knows the
   two shapes in **one place** (a small function that makes the target of each from alternatives).
4. **Is a part it would change cited** (the user: the worker does not change a part that something cites). Find
   how an update decides that a change needs `acknowledge_citations` (`pangloss_cypher/citations.py`) and ask
   it **before** any candidate is asked, if that can be asked without writing; if it cannot, the update's own
   refusal is the check (step 7). Such a head is left whole, its mark completed, logged.
5. **The candidates**, for the stand-ins that are left, **as the stored route asks them**
   (`candidates_of(plan, see_unpublished=True, limit=the dialog's limit of 10.5c, only=[...])`), in groups of at
   most `RECONCILE_MOST_UNKNOWNS` (check that `only` keeps a plan with more unknowns than that from being
   refused; if it does not, make it: a document with 30 mentions is asked in two groups, not refused). The fit
   in use read once for the batch. `suggest.decide_for` (10.5c) for each.
6. **A project's judge** is step 10.5c', not yours: leave one function between the decision and the write
   (`async def judged(...)` that returns the decision unchanged) so that 10.5c' has its place.
7. **One update**, if anything was decided: `rewriting.rewrite` with a change that replaces every decided
   stand-in, each suggestion with a **new id** (row 11; uuid7 as ids are made elsewhere), `text` the stand-in's
   label, `uris` its URIs, `identified_by` the toolchain's username and `toolchain_version` (what was used:
   the profile, and the fit's id if one decided "probably new"; short, one line), the target in the shape
   found, alternatives in the order decided with their per cent. Written **as the toolchain user**: the user of
   the write set for it (`current_request_username`, as the CLIs set it), the user made if absent (kind
   `toolchain`, with nothing to log in with, as the admin page makes one), and **refused, with the worker
   stopping and saying why, if `RECONCILE_TOOLCHAIN` names a person or a disabled user** (row 10). Based on the
   version read (`version=`). With `instructions={"suggest": False}`: **that takes the head's mark off in the
   update's own transaction** (row 19) and leaves no new one. Check what `less`/the vectors and criteria marks
   do with it: the update is an ordinary update for them, and marks them, which is right.
   - **A version that moved on** (`PanglossVersionConflictError`): nothing written; the claim released, the
     mark kept: tried again later if it is still marked (a person's save with `suggest=false` has taken it off;
     a person's later save with the setting on has marked it anew).
   - **Every other refusal** (a validation of the whole update, a citation, a `@before_update` that refuses,
     a reference gone): logged with the head, **the mark completed**, the stand-ins noted as tried (row 12).
   - **A failure that is not a refusal** (the database gone, a bug): the claim released, the mark kept, the
     error raised to the supervisor as the vectors worker does.
   - If nothing was decided: the mark completed by its token (`pending.complete`), no update.
8. **What it saw is kept for learning** (10.5f reads it; rows 2, 24), in the update's transaction if a hook of
   the write allows it without a second write that could be lost, else right after (say which, and what a crash
   between the two leaves): one `PGReconcileSuggested` for each suggestion written: its id (the new id), the
   head, when, `decisions.today(...)`'s record as `PGReconcileShown` keeps it, the rows as the stored route's
   record keeps them (`Found.best` with `displayed` = the list the decision was made on), the alternatives
   written. No user. **Not swept by age** (the sweep leaves them; it removes those whose suggestion is no
   longer a node of the kind: confirmed, or gone). `structure.py` and migration `0003`.
9. **Tried.** One `PGReconcileTried` for each stand-in left (its id, a digest of `decisions.today(...)`'s
   comparable record and of the settings of the decision): skipped while that digest is today's; removed by
   the sweep when its stand-in is gone. In the same migration. `pangloss reconcile suggest --again` (10.5e)
   will clear them: give the function now (`forget_tried(...)`).
10. **Waiting for the vectors** (row 14): a batch does not start while a rebuild is in hand, nor while there
    are marks of the vectors' kind **older than the oldest mark it would claim** (the writes that left its work
    have not reached the vectors yet); its own updates' vector marks are newer and are not waited for. If the
    vectors app is installed and its marks never go (no worker), it waits and `pending_status` says so.
11. **The label of the head after the rewrite** (row 18): a label made by a template from the field the
    mention was in may change when the mention becomes a suggestion (the name the source wrote is now `text`).
    Find what happens with the testing models' or the demo's templates, **decide the least surprising thing**
    (a label template that reads a stand-in's label should read a suggestion's `text` in its place, or the head
    keeps its label), build it if it is small and in reconcile or the library, and say it in the report with a
    test; if it needs the core's templates changed, stop at saying what is needed.
- `run(settings)` for the server (`api.py`: a `BackgroundTask` when `RECONCILE_WORKER_IN_SERVER`), `drain()`
  for tests, `pending_status()` (waiting, claimed, waiting for the vectors, the worker's lease), and the command
  `pangloss reconcile worker [--batch N]` (the other commands are 10.5e's). History records a version only in a

- a version conflict (the head saved by a person between the read and the write: make it with a hook or a
  patched step): nothing written by the worker, the mark kept, the next drain does it; a person's save with
  `suggest=false` meanwhile: the mark gone, the next drain does nothing;
- a stand-in tried is **not asked again** after a later save (count the calls of `candidates_of`), and is after
  the digest changes (a setting of the decision changed);
- **nothing is suggested twice**: two drains, and two workers' `process` on one head at once (the second finds
  no stand-in or a moved version);
