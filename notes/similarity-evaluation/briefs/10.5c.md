## What to build

### 1. The settings of the decision (`pangloss_reconcile/settings.py`, checked at `initialise()` as the others)

- `RECONCILE_SUGGEST_ABOVE` (0.6): the least score of the best candidate for a
  suggestion to be made. A number from 0 to 1.
- `RECONCILE_SUGGEST_MOST` (3): the most alternatives. A whole number from 1 to 10.
- `RECONCILE_SUGGEST_WITHIN` (0.7): an alternative scores at least this share of the
  best's. A number from 0 to 1.
- One constant for **the dialog's limit**, used by the interface's request, by `learning`'s "displayed" and by
  the worker to come (row 24: "none of these" is fitted on the list the dialog displays, so the worker decides
  on a list of that length): find where the five is said today (the interface module asks with a limit; the
  measurement scripts say `--shown 5`) and say it once in Python (`DISPLAYED = 5` or as fits), the interface
  reading it or a test holding the two equal.
- A function `suggest.decide_for(found, fit) -> Decision` (or in a module beside, if `suggest.py` should stay
  free of settings: your call) that makes `decide`'s arguments from an unknown's candidates as answered and the
  fit in use: `probably_new` is `learning.none_likelihood(...) > settings.new_above()` exactly as the stored
  route says `probablyNew` (one function for both, not a copy: look at `api.py`).
- The settings' docstring at the head of `settings.py`, `__all__`, `check()`.

### 2. An unconfirmed suggestion is not evidence (the user's decision, row 16)

What is below an `IdentificationByToolchain` (any class of suggestion, a project's or an outside toolchain's
too) is **not walked by the graph part and not counted in what a candidate is named with**, until a person
confirms it (it is then an `Identification`, another class, and counts).

- **A general hook in the core**, since neither `pangloss-fastrp` nor `pangloss-reconcile` may import the
  library and the library may import neither: `hooks.unconfirmed: list[Callable[[], Iterable[str]]]` with
  `unconfirmed_classes() -> list[str]`, in the manner of `references_not_counted`: the names of the wrapper
  classes whose nodes hold a guess that no person has confirmed; what is below one is not evidence of anything.
  The library registers a function that gives every class of suggestion (`IdentificationByToolchain` and what
  descends from it, by the registry, as `review.graph.suggestion_labels` finds them: each has its own label).
- **`pangloss-fastrp`**: such a class is an `END` in the maps (as `FastRPMeta(stop=True)`: a chain ends there
  with no join), whatever its own `FastRPMeta` says. That it changes **the graph part's signature** for a
  project that has such classes (a rebuild is then due) must follow from the maps being in the signature: check
  that it does, with a test, and say what a project sees (`pangloss vectors status`).
  The overlay and the refresh follow the maps: check with the part's suite (`pangloss_testing.parts`) that a
  document holding a suggestion gives the same vectors by refresh as by rebuild.
- **`pangloss-reconcile`, `reasons.py`**: a forward edge to a candidate, or to a shared entity, does not count
  when the node it starts from is, or is below (by the forward edges of its document, at any depth: wrappers
  nest, a `WeightedAlternative` under the suggestion under an `InRole`), a node of an unconfirmed class. Find
  the cheapest query that is right on both backends (`fragments` has helpers; Memgraph's `EXISTS` and
  variable-length matches can scan: read `implementation_docs/large-data.md` on what was found before); with no
  class registered the query text is **exactly today's** (no cost to a project without the library). Main
  measures it at size afterwards: say in the report which shapes you considered.
- Nothing else changes what "uses" an entity (lists of what is used, the check before a delete, typology's
  groups): only these two.
