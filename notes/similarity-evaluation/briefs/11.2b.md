## Why

What you saw and reported (people the document itself names come up at about 0.26 with a name score of 0) is a
fault of the form: what a candidate shares counted the same whoever the candidate is. Measured through the
service at size (Memgraph, 200 mentions; `planted_reconcile_byname_memgraph.txt`), the shared term **multiplied by
the name's cosine**:

| | Right first | Among five | Of names several people have, first |
|---|---|---|---|
| name 1, graph 0.25 | 66.5% | 95.5% | 53.9% |
| + shared at 0.75 (what is built) | 82.0% | 99.5% | 76.5% |
| + name's cosine × n/(n+2) at 1 | 85.5% | 99.5% | 79.1% |
| **the same at 1.5** | **86.5%** | 99.5% | 82.6% |
| at 2 / 3 / 4 | 86.5% / 86.0% / 85.0% | 99.5% | 82.6% / 81.7% / 80.9% |

## The change

1. `scores["shared"]` of a candidate is `lead × n / (n + 2)`, where `lead` is the candidate's cosine of **the part
   the weights weigh most** among the parts scored for that unknown (the highest weight; of several alike, the
   first by name; `shared` itself is not a part and never the lead). With the profile `who` that is the name. No
   part scored (the unknown has no vector of any weighed part): `shared` is 0. `reconcile` still names no part's
   package: the rule is about weights, not about "name". `score.py` stays without a database; say the rule in its
   docstring, with why (what is shared tells namesakes apart; it does not make someone of another name a
   candidate) and the figures above.
2. The default weight of `shared` is **1.5**. The default `RECONCILE_LEAST_SCORE` is **0.15** (with the default
   weights a candidate that shares nothing and whose name's cosine is 0.41 scores 0.15; phase 10's dialog cut at a
   name's cosine of 0.375).
3. The answer's `scores.shared` is that product (what the mean is of); the count itself is what `shared` (the
   list) shows. The interface's title text needs no change beyond what the number now is: check its tests.
4. Tests: the pure ones for the new rule (the lead chosen by weight, the tie by name, no part scored, a lead of 0
   gives 0 whatever is shared, weight 0 is still phase 10's score exactly). The planted floor again with the new
   form and weight (measure the three seeds; main's in-memory trial at 3,000 people gave 87 to 91% right first at
   weights 1.5 to 2; if at 1,500 people it is not clearly above your additive figures of 85.7 / 86.6 / 86.6%,
   stop and report). Live: your existing ones adjusted; **one new**: someone the document itself names, of
   another name altogether, who shares the document's other entities, scores under the least score and is not in

5. Docs: `reconcile.md` and the guide say the new rule and carry this table (keep the first table of the additive
