## Choices, as made

- One file, `packages/pangloss-names/tests/test_names_maths.py`, with no conftest: the tests are pure, and the planted
  test has no conftest either.
- I read `maths.py`, taking its docstrings as the rule, and `pangloss_models.text.fold`, which `fold` relies on for
  accents (`ß` to `ss`, `ł` to `l`, NFKD with the combining marks dropped).
- `DIMENSION = 1024` for the tests of nearness, so that hash collisions hardly move the cosines. The fixed places
  are written in at 256 and 1024.
- Fixed places, worked out once on 2026-11-21: `^jo` at 256 is (168, +1), `joh` (164, -1), `ohn` (37, +1),
  `hn$` (201, +1); at 1024, `^jo` is (680, +1) and `hn$` is (969, +1).
- The test across processes runs `sys.executable -c` twice, with `PYTHONHASHSEED` set to 1 and then 12345. It
  compares the JSON float lists of `vector('Johannes Mayer', Rarity(), 256)` exactly, with each other and with this
  process.
- The trigrams that collide (to test "add or cancel") are found inside the test, by searching `^ab`-style trigrams at
  dimension 8, rather than written in.
- The made-up population: each of 8 surnames given twice with `Johannes`, plus `Wilhelm Mayer` and
  `Wilhelm Schuster`. That makes 18 names, 16 of which contain `johannes`.
- Two small cases the brief did not list: `Rarity.of` takes a `Counter`, which is what `words` returns; and an empty
  `Rarity()` gives every word the same weight. The second is the premise of the "knows nothing" case.

## Departures and findings

- **"About as near" in the "knows nothing" case is not what happens.** With `Rarity()`, every word weighs the same,
  so `Johannes Schuster` is clearly nearer `Johannes Mayer` than `Wilhelm Mayer` is. The cosines are 0.62 and 0.40,
  because `johannes` brings 8 shared trigrams and `mayer` only 5. With the population's rarity it turns round to
  0.53 and 0.32. So the test asserts only the rule's main claim: with no rarity, `cosine(JM, WM) <= cosine(JM, JS)`.
  The parenthetical "about as near" in the brief cannot hold for these names and is not tested.
- I found nowhere that the code does not do what its docstrings say. All the cases in the brief pass as written.
