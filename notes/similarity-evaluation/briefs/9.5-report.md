## The demo's classes with dates (read from the models after `initialise()`, `/tmp` script: every class with a field
whose type is a `pangloss_dates` `Date`)

- **Documents with a date of their own** (`Statement` is `Dateable`, `date: Date`; some kinds say `Date | None`):
  every concrete kind of `Statement`: `Activity`, `Transfer`, `Inference`, `Naming`, `Gendering`, `MemberOf`,
  `ChildOf`, `Sibling`, `Order`, `CreationOfPhysicalObject`, `CreationOfArtwork`, `CreationOfPainting`. Each gets a
  time vector of its own (a statement is a document) and gives its date to its head.
- **The heads above them**: `Factoid` (`statements`, also inside `Negative`, `Hypothetical` and `Causes`) and
  `DerivedFactoid` (`statements`). Each has the dates of all its statements, added. All have vectors
  (`meta.model_included`: true); `Statement` is a group (`VectorsMeta(searchable=True)`).
- **An entity with a date of its own**: `Feast` (`DateCharacterisation.when`: `Recurrence | ExactDate | DateRange |
  EDTFDate | Period | NoRule`; `NoRule` is an `Embedded`, not a `Date`, so a feast attached by hand has none).
- **No date**: `Person` (its `born` went in migration 0021), `Source.written` (a `datetime.date`, not a
  `pangloss_dates` date: no time vector), `SamePerson`, the Zotero classes, `Place`, `Group`, the typology.
- **Questions for the user (nothing excluded)**:
  - `Feast`: its date is the calendar's (a saint's day every year as a `Recurrence`, whose vector is its
    lifetime only; a Lent as a `Period`), not when something happened. A recurrence with no start and no end runs
    from `DATES_EARLIEST_YEAR` (700 in the demo) to `DATES_OPEN_END_YEAR` (1600): longer than the ring (256 years),
    so it is at every place alike. `populate.py` makes no feast, so the demo's data has none until one is made.
  - A statement in `Negative` (said not to have happened), `Hypothetical`, or as an `Order`'s `thing_ordered`
    (in `PositiveSubjunctive` / `NegativeSubjunctive`: wished, not necessarily done): its date is when it did not,
    might, or was wished to happen, and it still counts for the statement and for the factoid at its head (the
    rule of 9.3: a date held by a document counts for it and its head, whatever the space above). An `Order`
    itself has its own `date` (when ordered) and not the ordered statement's.
  - `Inference`: a statement whose date is whatever the person writing it gives (when the conclusion is of, as the
    other statements; the model does not say).
