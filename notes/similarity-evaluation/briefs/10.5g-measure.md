## What to measure (each on Memgraph, then on Neo4j)

`planted_suggest_memgraph.txt` for the sizes then). The models need a place that **takes a toolchain's suggestion
of the class the worker writes** (`IdentificationByToolchainFromText`, the default of `RECONCILE_SUGGEST_AS`, and its

choose and say which, so that the existing tests of `suggested` are unchanged), with `Factoid` reviewed
(`reviewed=True`, `pangloss_review` installed) so that a written factoid is unpublished and the worker takes it. The
signature of the graph changes with the models: rebuild the vectors before measuring, and say how long that took.

A new command of `planted.py`, **`worker`**, on the loaded and rebuilt world:

1. **The push**: write `--push N` (2,000 by default) factoids, **unpublished**, each a copy of a mention's own
   factoid with the mention's person replaced by a `Mention` stand-in where the place takes a suggestion
   (`Activity.participants`), and in `carried_out_by` where it does not (as the world has it: say the share of
   each), saved with `?suggest=true` or `RECONCILE_SUGGEST_ON_SAVE` (say which), as a project's upload would, in
   batches as `load` does. Of every second, the person is taken out of the database (as `suggest` does: "someone
   new"), so the share of wrong suggestions for people not there can be counted. Time the push (per document), and
   what the marking costs it (the same push with the setting off).
2. **The worker apart** (`suggesting.drain` in this process, the vectors worker first run to its end, so that only
   the reconcile worker is timed): documents a minute, mentions a minute, the time of a batch by its parts if
   cheap (candidates, decision, the update with `still_mine`), the backlog's fall over time (a line each 30 s).
3. **The worker in the server**: the vectors worker and the reconcile worker in one process and one loop, as
   `RECONCILE_WORKER_IN_SERVER` runs them, from the push on: how far the reconcile worker lags the vectors worker,
   and the backlog of each over time; when the last suggestion is written. (Not through HTTP: the two workers as
   the server starts them; say how you started them.)
4. **What was suggested**: of the mentions whose person is in the database: a suggestion written, the person first,
   the person among its alternatives; of those whose person is not: a suggestion written all the same; left for
   no place; left for a low score or "new"; tried rows; alternatives per suggestion. Compare with "10.5c measured"
   (the same thresholds, 0.6/0.7/3, should give about the same shares: say if not, and why).
5. **The two waves**: push a second set of mentions (`--push`, other mentions) after the first wave's suggestions
   are stored and unconfirmed, and decide them; then the same second set on a database where the first wave's
   suggestions were never written (the first wave's factoids deleted, or the second asked first: choose the
   cheaper honest way, say which). The decisions should be the same (an unconfirmed suggestion is no evidence,
   10.5c): count the mentions whose decision or scores differ, and show one if any.
6. **Costs asked by the earlier steps**: (a) what `reasons.py`'s condition on unconfirmed classes costs a request
   at size (`planted.py suggest --unconfirmed` against without, as in 10.5c, a sample of 200); (b) `mark_stored`
   over every stored factoid (the trigger `pangloss reconcile suggest`: its page query, the whole time, marks a
   second); (c) the three statements `still_mine` adds to each update (the update's time with and without, on a
   sample); (d) a judge that answers `Unchanged()` at once (`RECONCILE_SUGGEST_WITH`): what it adds per mention.
