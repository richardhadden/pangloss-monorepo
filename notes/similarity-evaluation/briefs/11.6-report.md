## Choices, as they are made

1. **`fits.in_use()` reads one node.** It read every fit and took the first marked; now it asks
   `MATCH (m:PGReconcileModel) WHERE m.in_use = true ... ORDER BY at DESC, id LIMIT 1`: the same answer (the newest of
   those marked, two runs at one instant being possible), one small read, which the stored route now makes on every
   request. `fits.fits()` is unchanged.
2. **No index, no migration 0003.** Fits are written by hand, one a run of `pangloss reconcile learn` with something
   new: few nodes; a scan of the label is what reading them costs. `structure.py`'s text says so (it said "finding the
   one in use is a scan of the label", still true, now on every request of the stored route).
3. **11.5's writer made a function**, `fits.write(properties)` (the one write transaction that unmarks the fit in use
   when the new one is in use, and creates it), called by `learn` as before; the live tests plant a fit through it,
   as the brief asks ("through 11.5's own writer": there was none apart from `learn`).
4. **One read transaction more**, not put in a transaction the route already runs: the route's own transactions are
   the plan's, the parts', the reasons', `store.states()` and the reference views', all in other packages
   (`pangloss-vectors`, `pangloss-cypher`); none is this package's to add a statement to. It is read after the
   answer is made and what was offered kept, and only by the stored route.
5. **Where the logic lives**: `learning.none_likelihood(model, rows)` (no database: `Model.score` of `head_features`,
   None for no row, a row lacking a part the model weighs as `best_PART`, or a feature weighed that the head does not
   make); `fits.usable(fit, now)` (`of_today` and a "none of these" model: the small function that decides `used`);
   `api.none_of_these(given, fit, now, above)` (no database: builds `model` and adds `new`/`probablyNew`), called by
   the stored route after `keep_shown`, so the shown record is made of the answer without the figure (it never held
   it anyway: `rows_of` reads `Found`).
6. **`model.at`** is ISO 8601 (UTC), made from the node's datetime (Memgraph's naive one is taken as UTC, as
   elsewhere in the package). The keys of `model` are always the four; `model` is added at the end of the answer's
   object (the brief's "at the top" read as "at the top level").
7. **`probablyNew` is strictly above** the setting (equal is not), as "above" says; tested at the figure itself.
8. **`RECONCILE_NEW_ABOVE`**: refused like `RECONCILE_LEAST_SCORE` (a bool, a string, NaN, outside 0..1), at
   `initialise()` with the others; added to the live conftest's settings (the live tests' settings class is closed).
9. **The interface**: `probablyNewText(answer, id, language, limit)` in `candidates.ts` gives the percentage text (in
   the page's language, `style: "percent"`, no decimals) or null; null unless `probablyNew` is true *and* `new` is a
   number *and* `rowsOf` gives rows (so `pending` gives none). The component shows the line with `<Show when>` and the
   accessor; `title` from `t("reconcile_new_likelihood", ...)`. German "Wahrscheinlich noch nicht in der Datenbank" /
   "Zu {percent} keiner von diesen"; French "Probablement pas encore dans la base" / "{percent} de chances que ce ne
   soit aucun de ceux-ci".
10. **The e2e fit** is planted by Cypher (`helpers.fit_in_use`, beside `forget`): its six signatures copied from the
    `PGReconcileShown` the route wrote for the mention (so they are today's without the test knowing the vectors'
    signatures), a "none of these" model weighing nothing with constant 3 (95% of every list). 11.5's Python writer
    cannot be called from the e2e process (it needs the project's settings and backend). Taken away with
    `forget("PGReconcileModel")` before and after (a fixture), and in the dev-build test after the dialog is checked.
11. **Live tests' model** weighs `best` and `rows` (not `best` alone), so that `limit=1` and `limit=5` give different
    figures (with `best` alone they would be equal: the best row is the same).
