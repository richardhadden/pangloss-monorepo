## The step

Two ways to say that the items of a class have no vectors, neither of which needs the class's package to know
`pangloss-vectors` (both decided by the user):

- a project's setting `VECTORS_EXCLUDE = ["Note", …]`: classes by name, with their subclasses;
- the documents that stand in for an entity (`pangloss_models.hooks.stand_ins`; review's `Unreconciled` is the one
  today) have no vectors, unless the project says `VECTORS_INCLUDE = ["Unreconciled"]`.

## What main found and decided (build on it)

1. **Who asks today.** Everything goes through `meta.included(type_name)` (the store's `_rows` and combined vectors,
   the route's `excluded`) except `pangloss_vectors.pending_work_of`, which reads `vectors_meta(model).include`.
2. **The rule, in one function, `meta.included(type_name)`**, in this order:
   1. the class, or a class above it, is named in `VECTORS_EXCLUDE`: no (the project's last word, over everything);
   2. the class or a class above it declares `_vectors_meta` (nearest in the MRO, as now): its `include`;
   3. the class is a stand-in's document (the `document` of one of `hooks.stand_ins`, when that names a `Document`
      class), or below one: no, unless it or a class above it is named in `VECTORS_INCLUDE`;
   4. otherwise yes. A name that is no model's: yes, as now.
   `hooks.stand_ins` also holds generic wrappers (criteria's `MatchesCriteria`): those are not documents and are
   never items; the rule is only for stand-ins that are document classes.
3. **The settings are checked at `initialise()`** as `VECTORS_PROFILES` is (`check_profiles`, `after_finalise`),
   with `PanglossInitialisationError` that names the setting and the name: a name that is no model's; a name that is
   not a class of documents or entities; a name in both `VECTORS_EXCLUDE` and `VECTORS_INCLUDE`. Each a list of
   strings, else refused. Reading functions in `settings.py` like the others there.
4. **Excluded means "no vector is stored", and nothing else.** It does not take the class out of the joined graph
   (`FastRPMeta(include=False)` does that, and is not changed): the graph part still walks an excluded item and
   still computes its row, and the store leaves the row out, as it does today for `VectorsMeta(include=False)`.
   **Do not touch `packages/pangloss-fastrp`.**
5. **So a head of an excluded class is still marked for the worker.** A write of such a head can change the joins,
   and so the graph vectors, of the items it names. Today `pending_work_of` leaves no mark for `include=False`,
   which leaves those neighbours wrong until a rebuild: that is a fault, mended here: `pending_work_of` marks every
   head that is a document or an entity when the app is installed, whatever `included` says. (This departs from
   the plan's sentence "leaves no mark of the kind `vectors`": main's decision, and "As built" will say so.) The
   worker needs no change if the store drops the rows: check that it is so, with the test below.
6. **What is included is part of what a rebuild depends on**, for every part. One function (in `store.py` or
   `parts.py`: your choice) gives the signature a part is stored and compared with: the part's own `signature()`
   when no class of documents or entities is excluded, and otherwise `signature_of(part.signature(), [the names of
   the excluded classes, sorted])`. It is used wherever `signature()` of a part is stored or compared today
   (`rebuild.py`, `status.py`, `store.profile_signature`). So: a project with nothing excluded keeps its stored
   signatures; a class newly excluded (or a stand-in, once this is installed with review) makes a rebuild due, and
   the rebuild is what removes the old vectors. The reason shown by the status should stay understandable
   (it may simply be the existing "its signature is now…" text).
7. **Until that rebuild, nothing shows an excluded item**: the similar route leaves out a hit whose class is not
   `included` (where the hit's type is at hand most cheaply: in `nearest`, or where the route builds `results` from
   the reference views; say which). The route's `excluded` status for the item asked about is as now.
