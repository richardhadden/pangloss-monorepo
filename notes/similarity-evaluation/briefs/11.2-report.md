# Step 11.2: the builder's choices, written as they are made

(Brief: `11.2.md`. Branch `vectors-11-learn`; nothing committed.)

## Choices

1. `reasons.shared(plan, candidates, see_unpublished)` loses its `most`: it gives every shared entity of every
   candidate, uncut (most documents first, then id). The cut to `RECONCILE_REASONS` is `api.answer`'s, from
   `Candidate.shared`.
2. `Candidate.shared` is a tuple of `(entity id, documents)` pairs (the dataclass is frozen), default `()`.
3. `RECONCILE_LEAST_SCORE` is applied in `candidates_of` (so both routes, and any caller below them), **after
   ranking and before the cut to `limit`**: a request for 5 gets up to 5 above the floor.
4. `score.py` gains `SHARED = "shared"`, `shared_score(n)` and `with_shared(scores, n, weights)` (the part scores
   with `shared` added when its weight is above 0); `score()` is unchanged (the mean over the keys of `scores`).
5. `settings.weights()` adds `shared: 0.75` under what is given (`{**profile, "shared": 0.75, **given}`) and, as
   before, returns only weights above 0: `{"shared": 0}` gives phase 10's dict exactly. The refusal of a name reads
   "…which is not a part: what may be named is the parts (graph, name, time) and shared (the entities a candidate is
   named with)"; the "no part above 0" refusal adds "(shared alone is not enough)".
6. `RECONCILE_LEAST_SCORE`: a number (int or float, not bool) from 0 to 1 inclusive, refused otherwise with
   "RECONCILE_LEAST_SCORE is a number from 0 to 1 (the least score of a candidate given), not …"; checked in
   `check()` beside the others.
7. `api.answer` no longer takes the plan (nothing in it asks the database for reasons now).

## 11.2b: `shared` as the lead part's cosine × n / (n + 2) (brief `11.2b.md`)

1. `score.lead(scores, weights)` is the part of `scores` the weights weigh most; of several alike, the first by name.
   `shared` is never the lead, and with no part scored it gives None. `with_shared` then makes
   `scores["shared"] = scores[lead] × shared_score(n)`, or 0 with no lead. There is no clamp: a negative lead
   cosine would give a negative term. That is the brief's formula; cosines below 0 are rare and already in the mean.
2. Defaults: `shared` 1.5 and `RECONCILE_LEAST_SCORE` 0.15. A pure test checks that a name of cosine 0.41 sharing
   nothing scores 0.149, about 0.15.
3. Planted floor at 1.5, 1,500 people, the same mentions:

   | Seed | Graph alone | Additive at 0.75 | Product at 1 | **Product at 1.5** | Product at 2 | Namesakes first (1.5) |
   |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
   | 1 | 78.05% | 85.71% | 86.41% | **89.20%** | 89.55% | 77.9% |
   | 2 | 79.04% | 86.60% | 88.32% | **90.72%** | 91.75% | 81.9% |
   | 3 | 81.51% | 86.64% | 88.36% | **90.07%** | 91.10% | 81.2% |

   At 1.5 it is 3.4 to 4.1 points above the additive form on every seed, so I went on. Floors: first 0.88, among
   five 0.99 (100% on every seed), namesakes first 0.76, gain over the graph alone 0.08 (measured 8.6 to 11.7).
4. The new live test uses **Ben**, not Ada. In the live world Ada's name has a cosine of 0.236 with "Jon Smyth"
   (from the hashing of the names), so her shared term is 0.118 and she scores about 0.20 to 0.22: still over 0.15
   and still in the answer. Without `shared` she would be about 0.13. Ben (name cosine 0) shares Ada and the mill,
   gets `shared` 0, scores about 0.05 to 0.07, and is not in the answer.
5. The least-score test's middle threshold went from 0.4 to 0.3. The namesake is about 0.37 now, Ada about 0.2.
6. The e2e "named with 0.33" assertion is unchanged: the elder's name cosine is 1, so the term is 1 × 1/3. The
   interface's own tests use made-up numbers and needed no change.
7. Docs: the worked examples were recomputed (0.81 to 0.74 and 0.73; the guide's example 0.81 to 0.6). Both tables
   are in `reconcile.md`: the product form first, then the additive form as what was tried first. The stale
