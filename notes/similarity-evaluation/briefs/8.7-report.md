## Demo entities whose label is a name only loosely (a question for the user; nothing excluded)

`meta.named()` on the demo gives: `Artwork`, `Book`, `Feast`, `Group`, `OccupationType`, `Painting`, `Person`,
`PhysicalObject`, `Place`, `Source`, `ZoteroLibrary`, `ZoteroObject` (all by `label`). Not names of anyone in a
useful sense: `Source`, `Book`, `PhysicalObject`, `Artwork`, `Painting` (titles), `ZoteroObject` and `ZoteroLibrary`
(Zotero's items and libraries), `OccupationType` (the typology's types, e.g. an occupation word that may also be a
surname), and arguably `Feast` (a feast's name). `Place` and `Group` are names. They matter in two ways: an
unnarrowed search by a name finds them, and **the table of rarity counts their words too** (a word common in titles
weighs less in a person's name).
