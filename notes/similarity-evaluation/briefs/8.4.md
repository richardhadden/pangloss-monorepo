## What to build

`packages/pangloss-testing/src/pangloss_testing/parts/` (a package: `__init__.py` and what it needs), holding:

1. **The writes**, by name, on `pangloss_testing.similar_models` (moved out of `test_fastrp_worker_live.py`: `create`,
   `update_that_adds_a_reference`, `update_that_drops_a_reference`, `update_that_replaces_its_statements`, `delete`,
   `new_citation`, `criteria_change_candidates`, and the sequences delete then undelete, merge then its undo, publish
   then unpublish), with `write_shapes` and the payload helpers they need (moved from `fastrp_live_support.py`; the
   fastrp support module imports them from here so that its other tests are untouched). Add one write the name part
   needs and the graph part must also survive: **an update of an entity that changes its label** (a `Person`).
2. **The claims**, as functions a test calls with a part's name and its slow way. The slow way is
   `async def slow(db_driver) -> dict[str, list[float] | numpy array]`: the vectors a whole rebuild would give now, by
   item id (the graph part's is `fresh_oracle`; the class part's is `class_rebuild` of the worker test). The claims:
   - after each write, the other workers (criteria, derived) and **one turn** of the vectors' worker, the stored
     vectors of the part in use equal the slow way's to a tolerance (10⁻⁵ by default), **for exactly the same ids**
     (so nothing gone keeps a vector, and nothing there lacks one);
   - the same after delete/undelete, merge/undo, publish/unpublish under both values of `VECTORS_GRAPH_SEES`
     (`"everyone"`, `"logged_in"`);
   - **an overlay equals the same payload written**: `part.overlay(plan)` of a `Create` payload gives, for the ids it
     answers for, the vectors stored after that payload is written and the worker has turned (see how
     `test_fastrp_part_live.py` makes the plan and matches ids; a part answers for the head, or for more);
   - **a rebuild under writes loses none**: writes made while a (slowed) rebuild runs are all reflected after it and
     one turn (the worker test's last test is the model).
3. **How a package calls it.** One obvious way, of your choosing after reading the conformance suite (a function
   that makes the parametrised tests, or a base class a test module subclasses), such that a part's test module is
   short: it names the part, gives the slow way, the installed apps and any settings, and gets every claim as a
   test of its own, with a sentence for a name. Say in the report how a new part calls it, in ten lines.

Then:

- `test_fastrp_worker_live.py` **calls the suite for the graph part** (and, as today, checks the class part beside
  it), and keeps as its own only what is the graph part's alone (the cap, the batch in groups, the criteria worker's
  new candidate if it does not generalise). **It must assert what it asserted before**: do not weaken a claim to make
  it general; if a claim cannot be said for every part, leave it in the graph part's file and say so in the report.
- The class part is called through the suite too if that costs little (`packages/pangloss-vectors/tests/live/`),
  without removing what its own tests assert. If it does not fit, say why and leave it.
